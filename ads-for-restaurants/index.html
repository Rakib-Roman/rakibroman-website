FAQ

Restaurant marketing, answered.

How can a restaurant get more customers with Facebook ads?

Show short, appetizing videos of your food to people who live or work within a few miles, at the times they decide where to eat, usually late morning and late afternoon. Pair the video with one clear reason to come in, such as a weekday special, and send people to directions, a call button or your own ordering page.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for restaurants?

Yes, when they target a tight radius and use real food video. Food is visual, and Reels and Stories let you put a sizzling dish in front of hungry people nearby. They work best for filling slow days, launching new menu items and promoting events.

How much should a restaurant spend on ads?

Start with a small daily test budget focused on your slow days, measure calls, direction requests and orders, then scale only the ads that bring people in. The right budget depends on your average check and how many extra covers your kitchen can handle.

What makes a good restaurant video ad?

It opens with the food in the first second, ideally with sound like a sizzle or a pour, keeps it under 15 seconds, shows one dish rather than the whole menu, adds captions, and ends with one clear action like order now or get directions.

Should my restaurant run Google ads too?

Yes for search terms with intent, such as people searching for your type of food near them, and for your own name so competitors and delivery apps don't take those clicks. Social video creates demand, Google captures people already looking.

Can ads reduce how much I depend on delivery apps?

They can shift part of your orders to direct ordering and walk-ins. Ads that send people to your own ordering page or front door keep the commission you would otherwise pay the app.
