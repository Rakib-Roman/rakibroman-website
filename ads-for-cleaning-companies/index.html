FAQ

Cleaning company marketing, answered.

How do cleaning companies get more customers?

Show homeowners in your service area what your team actually does, with short before-and-after videos, then give them an easy first-clean offer and a quick quote form. Follow up fast, deliver a great first clean, and turn that customer into a recurring weekly or biweekly client.

Do Facebook ads work for house cleaning businesses?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram let you target homeowners in specific zip codes and show them satisfying before-and-after results. They work especially well for first-clean offers, move-out cleans and seasonal deep cleans.

Are Facebook ads better than lead apps for cleaning companies?

With your own ads the lead is yours alone, so you are not racing other companies to call first or competing only on price. Many cleaning companies use both at first, then shift budget toward their own ads as they find what works.

How much should a cleaning company spend on ads?

Start with a small daily test budget, measure quote requests and booked cleans, and scale the ads that bring in recurring clients. The value of a recurring client over a year is what tells you how much a new booking is worth.

What makes a good cleaning video ad?

A clear before-and-after in the first two seconds, real footage from a real home, captions, a face from your team to build trust, and one simple action such as get a quote.

Can you help turn one-time cleans into recurring clients?

Yes. I retarget people who booked a single clean with recurring plan offers and reminders, so the first job becomes a weekly or biweekly schedule.
