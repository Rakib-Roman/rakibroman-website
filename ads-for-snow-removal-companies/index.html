FAQ

Snow removal marketing, answered.

How do snow removal companies get more customers?

The companies that grow fastest sell seasonal contracts before the first storm instead of waiting for the phone to ring when it snows. Short video ads on Facebook and Instagram, shown to homeowners and property managers inside your service area, plus Google search ads for people typing "snow removal near me", fill the route early. A clear offer, such as an early-signup price, turns those views into signed contracts.

Do Facebook ads work for snow removal businesses?

Yes, when they target the right radius and the right moment. Facebook and Instagram let you reach homeowners in the exact zip codes you plow, show them a real video of your crew clearing driveways, and send them to a simple quote form. They work best for selling seasonal contracts in the fall and for filling open spots after the first big snowfall.

When should a snow removal company start advertising?

Start in September or October to sell seasonal contracts while people are planning ahead, then increase spend around the first forecasted storm, when demand jumps. Mid-season ads fill gaps in your routes. Waiting until it snows means competing with everyone else on price.

How much should a snow plow business spend on ads?

It depends on your service area, crew capacity and contract price. Most local service companies start with a small daily test budget, find the ad and offer that books contracts at a cost that fits their margins, then scale only that. I help you set a budget based on how many new routes you can actually service.

What makes a good snow removal video ad?

Real footage beats stock footage. A strong ad opens with a satisfying before-and-after of a buried driveway being cleared, shows your truck and crew working early in the morning, states one clear offer, and ends with how to get a quote. It needs captions, because most people watch with the sound off, and a vertical version for Reels and Stories.

Can you help sell seasonal snow removal contracts?

Yes. Seasonal contracts are the main goal. I build the offer and ads around signing homeowners and commercial properties for the whole season, so your revenue is booked before the snow falls instead of depending on each storm.

Do you work with commercial snow removal companies?

Yes. For commercial snow and ice management I focus on property managers, HOAs and business owners, using Google search ads for commercial snow removal, Meta ads aimed at decision-makers, and video that shows reliability, equipment and response times.
